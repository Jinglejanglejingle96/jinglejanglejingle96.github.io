J33V35 privacy policy

J33V35 is a private assistant used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reading messages, creating drafts and sending mail on the owner's request.
  • Google Calendar: reading and managing the owner's calendars and events.
  • The account's email address and basic profile, to know which account is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data from Google is used only to provide the assistant's features to its owner.
  • Access tokens and any data kept are stored on the owner's own server.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • J33V35's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
